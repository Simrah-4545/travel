Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common traveler questions regarding Skyscanner comparison & booking.

How does Skyscanner search work on BudgetStayFly?

BudgetStayFly connects directly to Skyscanner's meta-search engine. When you perform a search for flights, hotels, or car hire, we query over 1,200+ airlines and travel sites to show you the lowest available fares.

Are there any hidden fees or extra costs?

No. BudgetStayFly and Skyscanner are 100% free to use. We do not add any commissions or extra fees to your ticket price. You pay the exact amount displayed by the airline or hotel.

How do I search for flights 'Everywhere'?

Click on the 'Explore Everywhere' tab in the main search box, type your departure city, and click search. Skyscanner will rank destinations worldwide from cheapest to most expensive.

What happens when I click 'Book on Skyscanner'?

You will be redirected directly to Skyscanner's secure referral portal or the official provider site (such as Emirates, Booking.com, or Hertz) to complete your booking safely.
